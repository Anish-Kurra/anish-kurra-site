About me

I'm a sophomore at Wayzata High School in Corcoran, Minnesota. I co-founded Project ANA, a nonprofit working on eating disorders that go undiagnosed in men, and I'm a research intern at the University of North Dakota and the University of Iowa.

I'm also building Gibby, a physics-informed model that predicts Gibbs free energy and binding affinity. Outside of that, I write children's books, spend time with friends and watch tennis.

I'm always open to new conversations, so feel free to email me at anishkurra@gmail.com.
