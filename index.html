About me

I'm a sophomore at Wayzata High School in Corcoran, Minnesota. I co-founded Project ANA, a nonprofit working on eating disorders that go undiagnosed in men, and I'm a research intern at the University of North Dakota and the University of Iowa.

My research is in molecular biology. At North Dakota I study how histone acetylation and the EP300 protein affect cellular senescence, using R for differential expression analysis. At Iowa I model mitochondrial Cox gene structures in the New Zealand mud snail, Potamopyrgus antipodarum, to understand how new species form.

Outside the lab, I lead a department of journal editors at the International Youth Neuroscience Association (IYNA) and work as an executive assistant at Dr. Interested, a youth-in-medicine nonprofit.

I'm also building Gibby, a physics-informed model that predicts Gibbs free energy and binding affinity. Through Books4STEM I've written or edited more than ten children's books about math and medicine. When I'm not working, I spend time with friends and watch tennis.

I'm always open to new conversations, so feel free to email me at anishkurra@gmail.com.
