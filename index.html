I'm a sophomore at Wayzata High School in Corcoran, Minnesota. I'm interested in neuroscience, medicine and health literacy.

Right now I co-run Project ANA, a nonprofit working on eating disorders that go undiagnosed in men. I'm also a research intern at the University of North Dakota and the University of Iowa, and I'm building Gibby, a graph neural network that predicts Gibbs free energy and binding affinity.

Outside of that, I write children's books, spend time with friends and watch tennis.

The best way to reach me is email: anishkurra@gmail.com.
