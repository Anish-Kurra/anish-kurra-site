Gibby

August 2026 – present
Founder & Lead Developer

Gibby predicts Gibbs free energy (G, ΔG and ΔΔG) and binding affinity for molecules. I started it in August 2026 and I'm its founder and lead developer.

The model is physics-informed. It starts from a quantum-chemical calculation of solvation free energy, then corrects that estimate with a graph neural network and gradient-boosted trees. On molecules held out from fitting, its solvation free energy is within 0.60 kcal/mol on average across 107 solvents, and within 0.78 kcal/mol in water.

You can run Gibby as an extension inside ChimeraX, the molecular visualization program, or use it on its own website.
